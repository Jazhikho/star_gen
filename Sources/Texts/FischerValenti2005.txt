Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Fischer, D. A., & Valenti, J. (2005).
The planet-metallicity correlation.
The Astrophysical Journal, 622(2), 1102-1117.
PDF: https://www.astro.ucla.edu/~aes/AST278/reading/Fischer_2005_ApJ_622_1102.pdf
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1086/428383

What was reviewed:
- Abstract and source metadata on 2026-04-03.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The paper finds a strong empirical correlation between host-star metallicity and the occurrence of detectable giant planets.
- Metal-rich systems are more likely to form or retain giant planets than metal-poor systems under otherwise similar broad conditions.
- For StarGen, the practical value is that metallicity should not be cosmetic once aggregate planetary models are in play. It should affect giant-planet weighting and, by extension, the architecture of the whole system.

How StarGen uses this:
- As the scientific basis for letting system metallicity enrichment and metallicity-coupling strength alter giant-planet likelihood in the upstream planetary state.
- As support for treating metallicity as a downstream constraint on moon-rich giant systems and outer-system reservoir structure rather than leaving it as a viewer-only number.
