Herbort, O., Woitke, P., Helling, C., & Zerkle, A. L. (2024).
Habitability constraints by nutrient availability in atmospheres of rocky exoplanets.
arXiv:2404.04029
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04029

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper emphasizes that water-bearing environments can still be nutrient-limited, especially for phosphorus and trace metals.
- It is useful for keeping StarGen from equating condensates or solvent presence with sufficient biochemistry support in all cases.

How it is used
- Supports adding explicit nutrient-access penalties, especially for aerial or cloud biosphere scenarios.
- Supports keeping atmospheric or cloud-habitat life branches separate from surface-ocean assumptions.

Human review notes
- The current StarGen life model is surface-biased; use this source to justify a separate nutrient gate, not a blanket downgrade of all non-surface biospheres.
