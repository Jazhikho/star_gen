Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Lamy, P. L., Toth, I., Fernandez, Y. R., & Weaver, H. A. (2004).
The sizes, shapes, albedos, and colors of cometary nuclei.
In M. C. Festou, H. U. Keller, & H. A. Weaver (Eds.), Comets II (pp. 223-264).
PDF: https://physics.ucf.edu/~yfernandez/papers/comets2chapter/comets2reprint.pdf

What was reviewed:
- Chapter abstract/overview and source metadata on 2026-04-03.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- Comet nuclei are typically dark, low-albedo, irregular, volatile-rich bodies with low bulk density relative to stony asteroids.
- Their physical character is not well represented by treating them as ordinary asteroids with a different label.
- For StarGen, this supports distinct outer-system reservoir behavior and a real compositional split between asteroid-dominated and comet-dominated small-body populations.

How StarGen uses this:
- As support for increasing icy and primitive small-body outcomes in cold outer belts and comet-leaning reservoirs.
- As the scientific reason that comet-related small-body placement should respond to outer-reservoir strength and minor-body bias instead of being purely decorative.
