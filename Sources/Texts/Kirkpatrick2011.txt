## Abstract
We present ground-based spectroscopic verification of six Y dwarfs (see also Cushing et al.), eighty-nine T dwarfs, eight L dwarfs, and one M dwarf identified by the Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (WISE). Eighty of these are cold brown dwarfs with spectral types ≥T6, six of which have been announced earlier in Mainzer et al. and Burgasser et al.
