Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Kirkpatrick, J. D., Cushing, M. C., Gelino, C. R., et al. (2011).
The first hundred brown dwarfs discovered by the Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (WISE).
The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series, 197(2), 19.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.4677
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/0067-0049/197/2/19

What was reviewed:
- arXiv abstract and source metadata on 2026-03-28.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The abstract reports spectroscopic verification of L, T, and Y dwarfs in one survey sample, including objects as late as early Y.
- It presents the WISE color and spectral-type locus for M, L, T, and Y dwarfs.
- It explicitly treats late-T and Y dwarfs as a nearby, volume-limited census problem rather than rare curiosities.

How StarGen uses this:
- As support for exposing T and Y dwarfs as real procedural outcomes rather than stopping at L dwarfs.
- As the source-backed reason to color and classify substellar outputs across L, T, and Y rather than one generic "brown dwarf" label.
