Spiegel, D. S., & Turner, E. L. (2012).
Bayesian analysis of the astrobiological implications of life's early emergence on Earth.
arXiv:1107.3835
https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3835

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper is the cautionary counterweight to rapid-biogenesis optimism.
- It argues that early life on Earth, by itself, does not strongly prove that abiogenesis is easy on other worlds.
- In StarGen, this supports keeping the realistic default cautious and prevents the optimistic model from becoming the only interpretation.

How it is used
- Justifies the "Earth History" default and the idea that favorable habitability does not automatically imply high abiogenesis probability.
- Supports keeping separate controls for simple-life onset versus complex-life and civilization emergence.

Human review notes
- Use as a guardrail against overclaiming from the Earth example.
