### L and T dwarf classification — spectral type definitions
Establishes the L and T spectral class definitions for brown dwarfs. Temperature ranges:
L dwarfs ~1300–2200 K; T dwarfs ~700–1300 K.

**StarGen spectral classification:**
- `SpectralType` enum extension: `L` | `T` | `Y` (add to M-dwarf branch)
- L dwarf: Teff ≈ 1300–2200 K; detectable via methane absence, dust clouds
- T dwarf: Teff ≈ 700–1300 K; methane absorption prominent
- Apply in IMF sampling when mass < ~0.072 M☉ (hydrogen-burning limit)
