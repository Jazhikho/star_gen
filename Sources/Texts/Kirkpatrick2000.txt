Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Kirkpatrick, J. D., Reid, I. N., Liebert, J., Gizis, J. E., Burgasser, A. J., Monet, D. G., Dahn, C. C., Nelson, B., & Williams, R. J. (2000).
Sixty-seven additional L dwarfs discovered by the Two Micron All Sky Survey (2MASS).
The Astronomical Journal, 120(1), 447-472.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0003317
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1086/301427

What was reviewed:
- arXiv abstract and source metadata on 2026-03-28.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The abstract states that L dwarfs span roughly 1300 K to 2000 K in effective temperature.
- It also notes that the transition from the coolest L dwarfs to the warmest T dwarfs occupies a relatively small temperature interval.

How StarGen uses this:
- As the warm-end anchor for the brown-dwarf temperature ladder.
- As support for treating L dwarfs as a physically distinct output class instead of folding all substellar objects into late M dwarfs.
