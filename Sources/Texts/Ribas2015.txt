Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Ribas, A., Bouy, H., & Merin, B. (2015).
Protoplanetary disk lifetimes vs stellar mass and possible implications for giant planet populations.
Astronomy & Astrophysics, 576, A52.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00631
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201424846

What was reviewed:
- Abstract and source metadata on 2026-04-22.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The paper studies 22 nearby associations and a subsample of 1,428 spectroscopically confirmed members.
- It reports robust statistical evidence that protoplanetary-disk evolution depends on stellar mass, with disks evolving faster and/or earlier around stars above about 2 solar masses.
- The practical takeaway for StarGen is that gas-disk lifetime should not be treated as one universal scalar across all host-star masses.

How StarGen should use this:
- As support for making `DiskLifetimeMyr` a stellar-mass-aware prior in aggregate planetary generation.
- As a constraint on gas-giant formation and migration pressure around higher-mass stars, which should not inherit the same gas window as solar-mass hosts.
- As a calibration note that host-mass-dependent disk dispersal should be represented upstream in `PlanetarySystemState`, not only in late planet-level overrides.
