### Initial mass function — broken power-law (competing model)
Three-segment broken power-law: α₁ ≈ 0.3 (0.08–0.5 M☉), α₂ ≈ 1.3 (0.5–1.0 M☉),
α₃ ≈ 2.3 (> 1.0 M☉). Produces different low-mass end than Chabrier lognormal.

**StarGen IMF option:**

- Segment breakpoints: 0.08, 0.5, 1.0 M☉
- Exponents: 0.3, 1.3, 2.3 (these are α in dN/dm ∝ m^(-α))
- Key difference from Chabrier: Kroupa produces slightly more stars at 0.1–0.5 M☉.
