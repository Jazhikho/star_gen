### Planet occurrence rates — Kepler (primary demographic reference for FGK)
η_Earth (FGK): ~22% have rocky HZ planet. Also: nearly every Sun-like star has
a planet between 1–4 R⊕ at < 1 yr period. Occurrence peaks around 2 R⊕, 10-day period.

**StarGen occurrence calibration:**
- `OccurrenceRate_FGK` table (R_p vs P bins) from Table 3 / Figure 9 of paper.
- `EtaEarth_Petigura2013` ≈ 0.22 (HZ rocky planets per FGK star); large uncertainty.
- Occurrence as function of orbital period: power-law rise from 1 day to ~100 days
  for sub-Neptunes (dN/d log P ∝ P^α, α ~ 0).
