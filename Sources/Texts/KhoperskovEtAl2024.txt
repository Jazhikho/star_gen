## Abstract
There is no doubt that the Milky Way is a barred galaxy; however, factors that establish
its prominent morphology remain largely elusive. In this work, we attempt to constrain the
history of the MW by tracing the present-day parameters and evolution of a set of MW and
M31 analogues from the TNG50 simulations. We find that the strength of bars at z = 0
correlates well not only with the stellar mass build-up but, more crucially, with the time
of onset of stellar discs. Discs of strongly barred galaxies form early (z ≳ 2–3), compared
to weakly and non-barred galaxies (z ≈ 1–1.5). Although we are cautious to draw ultimate
conclusions about the governing factor of disc formation due to the complexity and
correlations between different phenomena, the observed morphological diversity can be
tentatively explained by a substantial variation in the gas angular momentum around
proto-galaxies already at z ≈ 3–5; in such a way, early discs formed from gas with larger
angular momentum. By comparing the formation timescales of discs of barred galaxies in the
TNG50 sample, we infer that the MW has a strong bar (0.35 < A₂ < 0.6) and that its stellar
disc started to dominate over the spheroidal component already at z ≈ 2, with a mass of
≈ 1 ± 0.5 × 10¹⁰ M⊙. We conclude that the presence of a strong bar in the MW is a natural
manifestation of the early formation of the stellar disc, which made possible bursty but
highly efficient star formation at high redshift.

## Major claims, findings, models, assumptions, and limitations
- **Bar strength correlates with disk formation epoch:** Galaxies with strong bars (A₂ >
  0.35) formed their stellar disks early (z ≳ 2–3, i.e., > 10 Gyr ago). Weakly barred
  or unbarred galaxies formed their disks later (z ≈ 1–1.5, i.e., 8–10 Gyr ago).
- **MW bar strength: A₂ = 0.35–0.60** (strong bar). This implies the MW disk began
  dominating at z ≈ 2 (≈10.5 Gyr ago) with a disk mass of ~10¹⁰ M⊙.
- **Early disk formation = early bar formation:** For the MW, the bar likely formed
  ~8–10 Gyr ago. Stars formed before bar formation have different kinematics (more
  radial, affected by bar resonances) than post-bar stars.
- **Gas angular momentum as root cause:** At z = 3–5, proto-galaxies that accreted gas
  with high angular momentum formed early disks → strong bars. Low angular momentum
  → late disk formation → weak or no bar.
- **Morphological diversity in galaxies:** The diversity of bar strengths across spiral
  galaxies (from very strong to absent) can be traced to the distribution of gas angular
  momentum at the proto-galaxy stage — a largely stochastic quantity at formation.
- **TNG50 validation:** MW and M31 analogues from TNG50 cosmological simulation provide
  statistical backing; not based solely on our Galaxy's single data point.
- **Limitation:** TNG50 simulation resolution; bar formation in cosmological simulations
  may be affected by numerical effects at subgalactic scales.
- **Limitation:** The correlation (early disk = strong bar) is statistical; individual
  galaxy variation exists. Using mean trend for StarGen is appropriate but implies
  scatter around the mean.

**Bar probability model for procedural galaxies:**
```csharp
// Probability of strong bar given disk formation epoch
// Khoperskov et al.: early disk (z > 2) → strongly barred; late (z < 1.5) → weakly/unbarred
double diskFormationZ = /* from galaxy generation */;
double barStrengthExpected;

if (diskFormationZ >= 2.5)
{
    barStrengthExpected = 0.40 + rng.NextDouble() * 0.20;  // 0.40–0.60: strongly barred
}
else if (diskFormationZ >= 1.5)
{
    barStrengthExpected = 0.15 + rng.NextDouble() * 0.20;  // 0.15–0.35: weakly barred
}
else
{
    barStrengthExpected = rng.NextDouble() * 0.15;          // 0–0.15: unbarred
}
galaxy.BarStrengthA2 = barStrengthExpected;
galaxy.HasBar = barStrengthExpected > 0.20;
```

**Bar formation age impact on stellar populations:**
```csharp
// Stars formed before bar formation (> barFormationAgeGyr ago) have
// different kinematic properties — higher radial velocity dispersion
// near resonance radii due to bar-driven scattering
if (star.AgeGyr > galaxy.BarFormationAgeGyr && star.GalacticRadiusKpc < 6.0)
{
    star.KinematicClass = KinematicClass.PreBarResidual;
    star.VelocityDispersionBoost = 0.15;  // 15% boost to radial σ near bar
}
```

**GHZ inner boundary adjustment for bar:**
```csharp
// Bar region has disrupted star formation and high stellar density
// Inner GHZ boundary extends outward if galaxy has a strong bar
double innerGHZBoundaryKpc = 4.0;  // baseline
if (galaxy.HasBar)
{
    innerGHZBoundaryKpc += galaxy.BarStrengthA2 * 2.0;  // strong bar → wider exclusion
}
```

## StarGen mechanics translation

### Bar formation epoch for procedural galaxy types
KhoperskovEtAl2024 establishes that bar presence and strength in spiral galaxies is
deterministic from disk formation epoch (itself a proxy for gas angular momentum at
galaxy birth). For StarGen procedural galaxy generation: when a galaxy's disk formation
redshift is sampled, bar strength follows predictably.

**Primary updates to StarGen galactic generator:**
1. Sample disk formation redshift from cosmologically-motivated distribution during
   galaxy generation
2. Derive bar strength A₂ from disk formation redshift (lookup or formula from Khoperskov)
3. Set `BarFormationAgeGyr` = disk onset age + 1–2 Gyr (bar develops after disk)
4. Apply kinematic boost to pre-bar stars in inner disk zones
5. Extend inner GHZ boundary for strongly-barred galaxies
