What was reviewed:
- Official Cepheus Engine SRD legal page on 2026-04-19.
- Official Cepheus Engine SRD Chapter 12: Worlds on 2026-04-19.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The legal page states that all text in the document is designated as Open Game Content except the product titles and the trademarks "Cepheus Engine" and "Samardan Press".
- The Compatibility-Statement License allows compatibility statements for Cepheus Engine when the required notices are followed.
- The worlds chapter exposes an openly usable UWP-driven world-generation flow: starport, size, atmosphere, hydrographics, population, government, law level, technology level, bases, travel zone, belts, gas giants, and allegiance notation.
- The chapter also ties star-map density to a one-parsec hex map and explicitly states the default one-half chance of a world in a hex, with modifier guidance for sparse, dense, and rift regions.

How StarGen uses this:
- Cepheus is the cleanest immediate candidate for a first-class RPG override because both the compatibility route and the relevant world-generation text are openly available.
- A future Cepheus override can safely produce UWP-compatible mainworlds, starport classes, travel-zone flags, belt and gas-giant counts, and parsec-friendly route metadata from the shared StarGen system and galaxy generators.
- Cepheus-style subsector density assumptions can also sit cleanly on top of the existing galaxy and jump-lane tools because the SRD already frames world placement in parsec hexes.
