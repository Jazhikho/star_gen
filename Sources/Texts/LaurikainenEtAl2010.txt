## Abstract
This paper presents 2D multi-component photometric decompositions for 175 early-type
disk galaxies (117 S0 lenticulars, 22 S0/a, and 36 Sa spirals), using deep Ks-band
(2.2 μm) near-infrared images from the NIRS0S (Near-Infrared S0 Survey). Galaxy
structural components — bulge (Sérsic profile), disk (exponential), bar (Ferrers),
and in some cases lens or ring components — are fitted simultaneously. The paper
derives photometric scaling relations (effective radius Re, effective surface brightness
μe, Sérsic index n, B/T, D/T, Bar/T) as a function of morphological type. Key results:
S0 bulges lie on the same photometric plane as ellipticals (Kormendy relation); S0 disk
scale lengths and surface brightnesses are similar to those of Sa spirals, supporting
the hypothesis that S0 galaxies are "faded spirals"; and bar fraction among S0s is
substantial, suggesting bars play a role in the morphological transformation from
spiral to S0. The paper provides the most complete NIR structural decomposition catalog
for early-type disk galaxies available as of 2010.

## Major claims, findings, models, assumptions, and limitations
- S0 bulges: follow the Kormendy (μe – Re) relation of elliptical galaxies; they are
  "classical bulges" (not pseudo-bulges), with Sérsic index n typically 2–6
- S0 disks: scale lengths h_disk and surface brightnesses similar to Sa spirals;
  this "disk universality" between S0s and spirals supports the faded-spiral hypothesis
  for S0 formation
- Sérsic index n: bulge n ranges from ~1–7; S0 bulges have median n ~ 3–4; Sa bulges
  median n ~ 2–3; later-type spirals n ~ 1–2 (pseudo-bulge territory)
- Bar fraction: ~30–40% of S0s show a photometric bar; higher than previously thought
- B/T range: S0: ~0.3–0.65; Sa: ~0.2–0.5; Sb: ~0.1–0.35 (NIR values; slightly higher
  than optical because NIR better traces old stellar mass)
- Disk scale lengths: h_disk ~ 2–8 kpc for L* galaxies; median ~3–4 kpc (NIR)
- Disk scale height: not directly measured (2D images); h_z/h_disk ~ 0.1 (typical
  thin disk ratio used in the literature)
- Limitation: sample is early-type (S0 to Sa); no late spirals (Sb–Sd) or irregulars
- Limitation: deep NIR images but for moderately nearby bright galaxies; sample may be
  biased toward more luminous, more massive systems
- Limitation: bar fitting is non-trivial; bar/T values have larger systematic uncertainty

## Parameters, constraints, and mechanics implications

**Sérsic index n by component and type (NIR, from paper figures):**
- S0 bulge:      n = 3.5 ± 1.5 (median ~3–4; classical bulge)
- Sa bulge:      n = 2.5 ± 1.2 (median ~2–3; mix of classical and intermediate)
- Sb/Sc bulge:   n = 1.5 ± 0.7 (pseudo-bulge territory; not covered by this paper)
  (Use OohamaEtAl2009 for Sb–Sd types)
- Disk:          n = 1.0 (exponential disk by definition in the fit)
- Bar (Ferrers): n_Ferrers ~ 1–2 (Ferrers profile, not Sérsic)

**Disk scale length (Ks-band, for L* galaxies at ~5×10^10 M_sun):**
- Median h_disk ~ 3.2 kpc (range 1.5–7 kpc)
- Mass scaling: h_disk ∝ M_star^0.3–0.35 approximately (from comparison with literature)
- No strong dependence on Hubble type between S0 and Sa (disk universality confirmed)

**Disk scale height:**
- h_z/h_disk ~ 0.10–0.15 for thin disk (from 3D galaxy modeling in the literature;
  this paper measures h_disk; h_z inferred from literature)
- For the thick disk component: h_z_thick / h_disk ~ 0.3–0.5
- StarGen `disk_scale_height_pc` default: h_disk × 0.12

**B/T ratio (NIR, more accurate for stellar mass):**
- S0: B/T ~ 0.45 (NIR; slightly higher than optical OohamaEtAl2009 value due to NIR
  tracing old stellar mass better, avoiding dust and young star bias)
- Sa: B/T ~ 0.35–0.40
- Note: NIR values are recommended as the default for stellar-mass-accurate generation

**Bar fraction:**
- S0: bar fraction ~35% (substantial; bars are common in lenticulars)
- Sa: bar fraction ~30%
- Implication: when generating early-type disk galaxies, apply ~30–35% bar probability

## Conflicts and follow-up work

**Direct follow-up and superseding work from the same group:**
- Salo, H., et al. (2015) ApJS 219, 4 — S4G (Spitzer Survey of Stellar Structure in
  Galaxies) multi-component decompositions at 3.6 μm for 2352 galaxies; dramatically
  larger and more uniform sample. Open access. arXiv:1503.07711. This is the modern
  replacement for many use cases of Laurikainen et al. (2010).
- Laurikainen, E., & Salo, H. (2017) A&A 598, A10 — further structural analysis for
  NIRS0S sample with updated bar metrics.

**Complementary NIR structural work:**
- Graham, A. W., & Worley, C. C. (2008) MNRAS 388, 1708 — photometric structural
  parameters and scaling relations of Seyfert galaxies and normal galaxy sample.
- Gadotti, D. A. (2009) MNRAS 393, 1531 — pseudo-bulge identification and Sérsic
  indices in SDSS; complementary optical study. arXiv:0810.1953.
- Fisher, D. B., & Drory, N. (2008) AJ 136, 773 — pseudo-bulge and classical bulge
  Sérsic index divide (n=2 as boundary); important for interpreting n distributions.

**Context for S0 formation:**
- The "faded spiral" hypothesis (disk universality between S0 and Sa supports this)
  is contested by the "red-and-dead" formation scenario via merging. The observational
  evidence in this paper supports faded spirals, but both pathways likely operate.

## StarGen mechanics translation

### NIR-based galaxy structural parameter defaults
Laurikainen et al. (2010) provides the gold-standard structural parameters for S0 and
early spiral galaxies in NIR, which better trace stellar mass than optical.

**Parameter defaults (S0 / lenticular):**
- bulge_sersic_n: sample from Gaussian(mean=3.5, sigma=1.5), clamp to [1, 8]
- bulge_radius_pc (Re): scale from stellar mass; for M_star = 5×10^10 M_sun → Re ~ 1.5 kpc
- disk_scale_length_pc: scale from stellar mass; for M_star = 5×10^10 M_sun → h_disk ~ 3.2 kpc
- disk_scale_height_pc: disk_scale_length_pc × 0.12 (thin disk ratio)
- B/T_nir: sample from Gaussian(mean=0.45, sigma=0.10), clamp [0.15, 0.80]
- bar_probability: 0.35 (35% chance of a bar for S0/Sa types)

**Hubble type to NIR structural parameters (lookup table):**
GalaxySubtype | B/T_nir_mean | n_bulge_mean | BarProb
S0 / S0/a    |  0.45        | 3.5          | 0.35
Sa           |  0.38        | 2.8          | 0.30
(Sb, Sc, Sd: use OohamaEtAl2009.txt optical values; not in this paper's sample)

**Disk universality rule:**
Do NOT make disk_scale_length_pc or disk scale height depend on B/T or Hubble subtype
(within S0–Sa range). Scale only with stellar mass. This is the paper's primary insight.
