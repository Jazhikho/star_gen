Lingam, M., & Loeb, A. (2018).
Is Life Most Likely Around Sun-like Stars?
arXiv:1710.11134
https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11134

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper argues that host-star class affects relative habitability through stellar lifetime, atmospheric erosion, and biologically relevant ultraviolet irradiance.
- It pushes against the idea that the most numerous cool stars should automatically be treated as the best life hosts.

How it is used
- Supports making host-star context an explicit part of the life model instead of relying only on present-day planetary environment.
- Supports distinguishing relative prospects for complex exposed biospheres across M, K, and G star contexts.

Human review notes
- Treat as a comparative-habitability framing, not as a hard exclusion rule for low-mass hosts.
