Forgan, D. H., & Rice, K. (2010).
Numerical Testing of The Rare Earth Hypothesis using Monte Carlo Realisation Techniques.
arXiv:1001.1680
https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.1680

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper is directly useful for a "microbes may be common, complex life may still be rare" model family.
- It treats Rare Earth style filters as something that can suppress intelligent civilizations even when terrestrial planets and simple life are not vanishingly rare.
- In StarGen, this supports the "Rare Complex Life" model.

How it is used
- Justifies stronger filters on complex ecosystems and sentient or technological lineages.
- Supports leaving biospheres possible while making civilization-bearing worlds much less frequent.

Human review notes
- Use as a scenario model, not as settled consensus.
