Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Lambrechts, M., & Johansen, A. (2012).
Rapid growth of gas-giant cores by pebble accretion.
Astronomy & Astrophysics, 544, A32.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.3030
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201219127

What was reviewed:
- Abstract and source metadata on 2026-04-19.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The paper shows that cm-scale pebble accretion can accelerate giant-core growth enough to ease the usual disk-lifetime timescale problem.
- The practical takeaway is that a system can be biased toward faster giant formation without changing every other part of the planet generator.
- This gives StarGen a real named alternative to the standard heavy-core-first weighting.

How StarGen uses this:
- As the named support for the `Pebble-assisted` giant-growth option in aggregate planetary priors.
- As the basis for a higher giant-growth weighting when the upstream planetary profile assumes faster solid-core assembly.
