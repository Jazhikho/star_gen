Balbi, A., & Frank, A. (2023).
The Oxygen Bottleneck for Technospheres.
arXiv:2308.01160
https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01160

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper argues that technological civilizations may require stronger atmospheric oxygen conditions than complex life alone.
- In StarGen, this is useful for separating "complex biosphere present" from "native civilization likely."

How it is used
- Supports requiring breathable or oxygen-rich conditions as an important prior for higher sentience or civilization chances in the stricter life models.
- Helps justify why some worlds can host rich ecosystems without commonly reaching technological civilization.

Human review notes
- Use as a bottleneck prior for technospheres, not as a claim that oxygen is the only imaginable path to advanced life.
