Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- Tomkin Press states that, for commercial works, the complete text of the Starforged Reference Guide is licensed under CC BY 4.0, including rules summaries, moves, and oracles, and that the handouts and asset-card text are also licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- The same licensing page states that Tomkin Press images, icons, trade dress, and official-product implication are not permitted.
- The Rules & Setting Primer is a free official preview that includes the first chapter with rules and setting details.
- The Truths Workbook is a free official setting-building worksheet focused on deciding the condition of the Forge and the backdrop of challenges that drive adventures.

How StarGen uses this:
- Starforged is a defensible override target because the commercially usable text basis for the Reference Guide and handouts is explicitly open under CC BY 4.0.
- A future Starforged override can safely bias generation toward frontier pressure, challenge-rich planets and settlements, faction tension, and oracle-friendly hooks while staying setting-light and original.
- Any StarGen use of Starforged material must stay text-only, avoid Tomkin Press art or trade dress, and avoid implying official status.
