Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- Paizo's compatibility FAQ states that the compatibility license does not itself grant the right to use Starfinder mechanics or setting content; mechanics are generally provided under ORC or OGL in the books, while setting material is generally restricted.
- The compatibility page states that products must be fully compatible if they claim compatibility and must not imply endorsement or responsibility by Paizo.
- The free official Starfinder Second Edition Playtest Rulebook describes Starfinder as a futuristic science-fantasy game line designed for compatibility with Pathfinder's newer rules engine.

How StarGen uses this:
- Starfinder can be approached as a clean-room compatibility override, but not as a direct copy of proprietary setting tables or Pact Worlds content under this source set alone.
- A future Starfinder override should target broad science-fantasy output assumptions that are compatible with the public game identity: higher-tech worlds, more adventure-ready settlements, wider acceptance of unusual ancestries and environments, and stronger support for mixed tech and magic.
- If StarGen later needs exact Starfinder-facing tables or rule-specific mappings beyond these broad assumptions, a human-reviewed ORC-designated primary source should be added first.
