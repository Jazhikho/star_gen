Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Tokovinin, A. (2021).
Architecture of hierarchical stellar systems and their formation.
Universe, 7(9), 352.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09118
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/universe7090352

What was reviewed:
- arXiv abstract and source metadata on 2026-03-28.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The abstract reviews statistical trends in hierarchical stellar systems.
- It highlights orbit alignment and the tendency for stars in the same hierarchy to have comparable masses.
- It frames hierarchical systems as families with recognizable structural patterns rather than arbitrary random trees.

How StarGen uses this:
- As the justification for building nested binary architectures intentionally instead of pairing stars at random until one root node remains.
- As support for giving multi-star systems progressively wider outer orbits and more family-like mass groupings.
