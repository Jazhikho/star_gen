Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Rodriguez, S., & Padilla, N. D. (2013).
The Intrinsic Shape of Galaxies in SDSS/Galaxy Zoo.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3264
DOI: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1306.3264

What was reviewed:
- arXiv abstract and source metadata on 2026-04-19.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The paper estimates intrinsic 3D galaxy shapes using SDSS and Galaxy Zoo morphology data.
- It reports mean disc ellipticity for spirals and axis-ratio behavior for ellipticals, showing that galaxy flattening and roundness are measurable population properties rather than pure visual style.
- It also notes that shape depends on luminosity, size, and morphology, which fits StarGen's use of ellipticity as a broad structural control instead of a fixed constant.

How StarGen uses this:
- Supports the ellipticity control as a real structure parameter for disc and elliptical families.
- Supports tightening the tooltip/help wording so ellipticity is presented as a simplified intrinsic-shape proxy rather than a cosmetic stretch knob.
