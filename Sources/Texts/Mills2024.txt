Mills, D. B., Macalady, J. L., Frank, A., & Wright, J. T. (2024).
A reassessment of the "hard-steps" model for the evolution of intelligent life.
arXiv:2408.10293
https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10293

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper argues that the long delay before human intelligence on Earth may reflect environmental timing windows rather than a sequence of intrinsically improbable "hard steps."
- In StarGen, this is useful because it gives us a model where long-lived stable windows matter more than raw luck alone.

How it is used
- Supports the "Environmental Windows" life-potential model.
- Justifies giving extra weight to long stable habitable periods, breathable atmospheres, moderate radiation, and sustained surface diversity when evaluating complex life and civilizations.

Human review notes
- Use as a research-backed alternative framing, not as a final answer to the hard-steps debate.
