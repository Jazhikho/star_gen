Source note for StarGen ruleset and licensing review

Citation:
Mongoose Publishing. (2026).
Traveller Licensing.
https://www.mongoosepublishing.com/pages/traveller-licensing

What was reviewed:
- Official Traveller licensing page on 2026-04-19.
- Existing repository clean-room notes in `Docs/TravellerWorldCreation.md`.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- Mongoose describes three publishing paths: the Fair Use Policy for non-commercial fan material and small game aids, the TAS Programme for commercial material tied to the current edition and Charted Space, and the Traveller Compatibility Licence for original-universe commercial works derived from a growing system reference document.
- The Traveller Compatibility Licence explicitly excludes the Charted Space universe and is aimed at generic or wholly original universes.
- Mongoose states that the Traveller Compatibility Licence is currently only being accepted from established publishers.

How StarGen should use this:
- StarGen can continue to support a clean-room Traveller-style compatibility mode for generation overrides, UWP-style readouts, and route-friendly output without relying on Charted Space setting material.
- StarGen should not import or present official Traveller setting lore, logos, or endorsement language under this source alone.
- Future Traveller-compatible overrides should remain framed as compatibility-oriented output unless a separate human-reviewed publishing arrangement changes the licensing position.
