Source note for StarGen research review

Citation:
Mordasini, C., Alibert, Y., Benz, W., & Naef, D. (2007).
Giant planet formation by core accretion.
Extreme Solar Systems, ASP Conference Series.
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.5667

What was reviewed:
- Abstract and source metadata on 2026-04-19.

Key implementation notes used in StarGen:
- The paper reviews the standard giant-planet core-accretion picture, where a heavy solid core forms first and runaway gas accretion follows later.
- The practical takeaway for StarGen is that `Core Accretion` should remain the conservative baseline giant-growth model.
- The review also supports using synthetic-population style weighting rather than requiring a full planet-formation simulation.

How StarGen uses this:
- As the named support for the `Core Accretion` giant-growth option in aggregate planetary priors.
- As the baseline lower giant-growth weighting when compared with the faster pebble-assisted branch.
