## StarGen mechanics translation

### Planetary system inclination distribution / mutual inclinations
Kepler multi-planet systems show low mutual inclinations: Rayleigh distribution with
σ ≈ 1–2° for the bulk of the population; a second, high-inclination population exists.

**StarGen inclination implementation:**
- `MutualInclinationModel = Rayleigh`
- `Rayleigh_sigma_deg` = 1.5 (default for compact multi-planet systems)
- Two-component model: ~80% of systems at σ ≈ 1.5°, ~20% at σ ≈ 10° (dynamically hot).
- Apply to orbital plane assignment of each planet relative to first planet's plane.
