Olson, S. L., Jansen, M., & Abbot, D. S. (2020).
Oceanographic Considerations for Exoplanet Life Detection.
arXiv:1909.02928
https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.02928

Why this source matters for StarGen
- This paper argues that ocean dynamics and nutrient upwelling can materially change biosphere productivity and biosignature strength.
- It highlights that not all water worlds are equally productive simply because they possess liquid oceans.

How it is used
- Supports adding a nutrient-cycling or ocean-mixing proxy instead of using only raw ocean coverage in life and biosignature calculations.
- Supports giving some weight to rotation, pressure, and seasonal mixing context when estimating surface biosphere productivity.

Human review notes
- Use as a productivity and detectability constraint, not as a binary life/no-life cutoff.
