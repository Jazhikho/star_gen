## Abstract
This paper presents PARSEC: the PAdova and TRieste Stellar Evolution code, a
significantly updated version of the Padova stellar evolution library. Key updates
include: new solar chemical abundances (Caffau et al. 2011, Z_sun = 0.01524), a revised
equation of state, new opacity tables (OPAL + Marigo low-T tables), new nuclear
reaction rates, and new convective overshooting prescription. The code provides
stellar tracks and isochrones from 0.1 to 12 solar masses (subsequently extended),
covering the full evolutionary path from pre-main-sequence through the TP-AGB phase.
The updated solar model, revised helium-to-metal enrichment ratio (ΔY/ΔZ ≈ 1.78),
and improved mass loss prescriptions produce better agreement with observed CMDs of
star clusters and the solar properties.

## Major claims, findings, models, assumptions, and limitations
- Solar abundances: Caffau et al. (2011) Z_sun = 0.01524, Y_sun = 0.2485
- Helium enrichment law: Y = 0.2485 + 1.78Z (primordial + metal enrichment)
- Convective overshooting: step-overshoot with λ_ov = 0.5 H_p for M > ~1.5 M_sun
- Mass range covered: 0.1–12 M_sun in initial release; low-mass end well-calibrated to
  solar and sub-solar observations
- Evolutionary phases: pre-MS, MS, sub-giant, RGB, He-flash, HB/CHeB, E-AGB, TP-AGB
- Mass loss prescriptions: Reimers on RGB, Bloecker on AGB
- Isochrone grid: ages 0.1 Myr to ~20 Gyr; metallicities Z = 0.0001 to 0.06
  (equivalent [M/H] from −2.2 to +0.5 dex)
- Limitation: TP-AGB treatment simplified at this stage (improved in later PARSEC versions)
- Limitation: rotation not included; only classic overshoot model

## StarGen applicability
PARSEC isochrones are the foundational stellar evolution library for StarGen's stellar
generation: main-sequence lifetime, luminosity class, spectral type, post-MS evolution
track selection, and age-metallicity-mass relations. Used by stellar generator to place
stars on the HR diagram and assign consistent radius/luminosity/temperature.

## Parameters, constraints, and mechanics implications

**Main-sequence lifetime (T_MS) scaling:**
- T_MS ≈ (M/L) × 10^10 yr in solar units; PARSEC tracks give exact numbers by grid lookup.
- For solar-type star (1 M_sun, Z_sun): T_MS ≈ 10 Gyr.
- For 0.5 M_sun: T_MS ~ 50–70 Gyr; for 2 M_sun: T_MS ~ 1.5 Gyr.
- General power-law approximation: T_MS ∝ M^(-2.5) works to first order but deviates
  at low and high masses — use isochrone grids rather than power laws in StarGen.

**Mass-luminosity-temperature (ZAMS):**
- L/L_sun ≈ (M/M_sun)^4 for M = 1–30 M_sun (approximate)
- T_eff increases with M; decreases with Z (metal-rich stars cooler at given M)
- PARSEC provides L, T_eff, log g, R directly for each (M, age, Z) point

**Metallicity effect on stellar properties:**
- Higher Z → lower T_eff at fixed M (opacity increases, cooler surface)
- Higher Z → higher L on RGB; stronger mass loss on AGB
- Very metal-poor stars (Z < 0.0001): hotter, faster evolution, shorter T_MS at given M

**Age-metallicity tie to generation:**
- StarGen stellar generator must sample (M, Z, age) consistently from a joint distribution
  constrained by the galactic chemical evolution model (see SpitoniEtAl2017, HackshawEtAl2024)
- Cannot independently sample M, Z, age; older galactic populations are more metal-poor
  on average

**Post-MS evolution selection:**
- RGB, HB, AGB: probability of catching a star in each phase scales inversely with that
  phase duration relative to MS duration (~10^-4 to ~10^-2 of MS lifetime)
- White dwarf remnants: M < ~8 M_sun → WD; M = 8–20 M_sun → NS; M > ~20 M_sun → BH
  (PARSEC tracks give the pre-WD endpoint mass)

## Conflicts and follow-up work

**Significant updates to PARSEC (required follow-up):**
- **Chen et al. (2014) PARSEC v1.2 / v1.2S** — Extended mass range to 350 M_sun; improved
  intermediate-mass and massive star treatment; TP-AGB improvements (Marigo et al. 2013).
  arXiv:1409.6174. This is the version most commonly used in CMD 2.x web tools.
- **Nguyen et al. (2022) PARSEC v2.0** — Major revision: new convection scheme, improved
  overshooting, rotation (optional), updated nuclear networks. arXiv:2211.01816. MNRAS 519,
  2537. Substantially supersedes the 2012 release for massive stars; low-mass star
  treatment also revised.
- **Tang et al. (2014)** — Improved TP-AGB for PARSEC tracks. arXiv:1402.0235.
- The CMD 3.x web tool (http://stev.oapd.inaf.it/cmd) always provides the latest PARSEC
  version and is the authoritative source for isochrone tables.

**Competing/alternative libraries:**
- MIST (Dotter et al. 2016, Choi et al. 2016): MESA-based; includes rotation; commonly
  used alternative to PARSEC. arXiv:1601.05779.
- BaSTI (Pietrinferni et al. 2004, 2021): Bologna-based; good for old stellar populations.
- Dartmouth (Dotter et al. 2008): well-tested for low-mass stars and globular clusters.
- For StarGen: PARSEC is a good default given its broad mass/metallicity coverage; expose
  a library selector if scientific precision matters for a use case.

**Citation discovery:**

## StarGen mechanics translation

### Stellar evolution library selector
PARSEC provides the grid backing for stellar mass → {T_eff, L, R, phase, lifetime} lookups.

**EvolutionLibrary selector:**
- `PARSEC2012` — 2012 baseline (this paper); mass range 0.1–12 M_sun; good for most
  systems but TP-AGB and massive stars less accurate.
- `PARSEC2022` (recommended default) — v2.0 (Nguyen et al. 2022); broader mass range,
  better overshooting and convection. Use when the PARSEC v2.0 grid is integrated.
- `MIST2016` — alternative if user wants rotation-included tracks.

**Key numeric thresholds for StarGen stellar generator:**
- Solar reference: Z_sun = 0.01524, Y_sun = 0.2485
- MS lifetime (approximate, for generation-mode use): T_MS ≈ 10 Gyr × (M/M_sun)^-2.5
  (use grid for precision; this formula breaks below 0.5 M_sun and above 5 M_sun)
- Remnant boundary (PARSEC-consistent): M_initial < 8 M_sun → WD; 8–20 → NS; >20 → BH
- He enrichment: Y = 0.2485 + 1.78 × Z (used to set stellar interior Y from Z)

**Decision rule: evolved star probability**
For a star of age t and mass M, the probability it is still on the MS is:
  p_MS = 1 if t < T_MS(M, Z); else it has evolved off MS.
Post-MS phase assignment: interpolate from PARSEC grid by (M, Z, age).
StarGen should implement this as grid lookup, not analytic formula, for any mode
claiming scientific accuracy.

**User lever:** `EvolutionLibrary` enum controls which grid is loaded at generation time.
